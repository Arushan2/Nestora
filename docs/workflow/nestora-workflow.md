# Nestora Project Workflow

## Overview
Nestora is a home service management platform with three roles:
- Customer
- Service Provider
- Product Seller

It combines product e-commerce, service discovery, service booking, inquiry handling, ordering, payments, and ratings.

## Customer Workflow

### Authentication
- Sign up
- Sign in

### Browse and Engage
- View available products
- View available service providers
- Like products
- Add products and services to favourites
- View liked products
- View favourite services

### Product Path
- Select products
- Add to cart
- Place order
- Pay
- View order history
- Rate product

### Buy Now Path
- Select product
- Buy now
- Pay

### Service Path
- Inquire now
- Contact service provider
- Track inquiries
- Accept offer
- Pay

## Service Provider Workflow

### Onboarding
- Register
- Sign in
- Complete business profile
- Get verified by platform

### Listings Setup
- Add service listings
- Set pricing model
- Set availability calendar

### Inquiry and Offer Handling
- Receive customer inquiry
- Review inquiry details
- Contact customer
- Prepare cost estimation
- Generate and share estimation
- Send service offer to customer

### Post-Acceptance
- If offer is accepted, confirm and schedule work
- Carry out on-site work
- Mark service as complete
- If offer is rejected, revise and resend the offer

### Post-Completion
- Receive and respond to customer reviews

## Product Seller Workflow

### Onboarding
- Register
- Sign in
- Complete seller profile
- Get verified by platform

### Listings Setup
- Add product listings
- Set pricing
- Set stock quantity
- Publish product to store

### Order Handling
- Receive new order notification
- Review order details
- If order can be fulfilled, confirm and accept order
- Pack the order
- Dispatch and update tracking
- Notify customer of shipment
- Update stock after dispatch
- Receive payment
- If order cannot be fulfilled, decline or handle accordingly

### Post-Fulfillment
- Restock when inventory is low
- View sales and order history
- Receive and respond to product ratings

## Core Functional Areas
- Authentication
- Product browsing
- Service browsing
- Order management
- Offer management
- Scheduling
- Inventory
- Shipping
- Payments
- Reviews and ratings
- Verification

## Workflow Notes
- Customers can interact with both products and services from the same platform.
- Service providers and sellers must be verified before going live.
- Payment flows exist for both products and accepted service offers.
- Stock updates happen after dispatch and during restocking.
- Review responses are part of the post-completion and post-fulfillment flows.
