Abstract
Nestora is a web-based integrated platform designed to streamline interactions among
customers, service providers, product sellers, and administrators within a centralized digital
environment. The system was developed in response to the inefficiencies of traditional
fragmented approaches, where communication, scheduling, order processing, and platform
supervision are often handled through disconnected or manual methods. Such practices
frequently lead to delays, reduced transparency, poor coordination, and limited user
convenience. To address these challenges, Nestora provides a structured solution that
supports secure user registration, one-time password verification, profile management,
service and product listings, inquiries, bookings, inventory handling, order fulfillment, and
administrative oversight.
The platform is organized around distinct user roles, each assigned specific responsibilities
and permissions. Customers can browse available services and products, submit inquiries,
place orders, and monitor their activities through a unified interface. Service providers are
able to manage service listings, respond to customer inquiries, and coordinate appointments
or project schedules. Product sellers can control inventory, track stock availability, and
process orders efficiently. Administrators oversee the verification of users and the general
operation of the system to maintain reliability, security, and consistency across the platform.
Additional features such as calendar synchronization and payment gateway integration
further enhance the practicality and usability of the system.
Nestora was implemented using a modern full-stack technology stack consisting of React.js,
Tailwind CSS, shadcn user interface components, PHP, and MySQL. These technologies
were selected to ensure responsiveness, scalability, maintainability, and secure data
management. The system was tested and evaluated to confirm that it meets its intended
functional and usability requirements. Overall, Nestora offers a practical and efficient
solution for managing service-based and product-based interactions through a single, user-
friendly digital platform.

1. Introduction
1.1 Project Title
Nestora - Integrated Home Service System
1.2 Project Description
Nestora is a comprehensive digital platform designed to bridge the gap between
customers, service providers, and product sellers. Unlike traditional e-commerce sites,
Nestora manages the entire lifecycle of both physical products and professional
services. Customers can purchase home goods or book services such as buildings,
repairs or maintenance through a single, unified interface.
The system features an advanced inventory management module that tracks stock levels
in real-time and monitors product expiry dates to reduce waste. For service providers,
Nestora offers a structured inquiry-to-fulfillment workflow, supported by a dedicated
scheduling calendar to ensure no appointments are double-booked. To maintain high
standards, the platform includes a mandatory verification process where admins must
approve sellers and providers before they can list their offerings.
1.3 Background and Motivation
In Sri Lanka, many households struggle to find reliable home construction services and
quality home products through a single, trusted channel. Traditional methods of finding
service providers rely on word-of-mouth, which is often inefficient and lacks
transparency regarding quality and availability. Meanwhile, local sellers of home
products face challenges in managing inventory effectively, leading to stockouts or the
sale of near-expiry goods without proper promotion.
According to global digital commerce trends, consumers are increasingly seeking
"super-apps" that integrate service booking with product purchasing to save time and
ensure security. Nestora was conceptualized to address these challenges by providing a
unified digital infrastructure where service inquiries can be managed, product stock can
be tracked automatically, and payments are held securely until fulfillment is confirmed.
The motivation for this project is driven by the need to professionalize the home service
sector while empowering small-scale sellers with sophisticated inventory and analytics
tools.

1.4 Problem in Brief
Digital solutions in Sri Lanka are fragmented, offering basic web stores or static
directories rather than systems that manage end-to-end workflows. This gap forces
small businesses into time-consuming manual calendar tracking, while retailers operate
without automated monitoring for low stock or expiring goods. For customers, this
fragmentation introduces severe time inefficiencies and stress, as sourcing reliable help
is slow, and transacting with unverified individuals creates trust and security concerns.
Ultimately, these compounding operational bottlenecks stifle local business growth,
waste productive working hours, and leave homeowners without a reliable, efficient
maintenance infrastructure.
1.5 Proposed Solution
Nestora addresses these gaps through a centralized platform designed to maximize
operational efficiency, deliver major time-saving benefits for all users, support service
inquiry workflows, track real-time inventory, and secure financial transactions.
The following key features are included:
• Registration Verification System: Admins review and approve service provider
and seller registrations before listings go live to ensure platform quality.
• Integrated Inventory: Automatic stock deduction, low-stock alerts, and an
automated expiry date detection system to reduce product waste.
• Inquiry-to-Offer Workflow: A structured system to route customer inquiries,
track statuses, and link accepted offers to scheduling.
• Secure Payment Holding: A "hold and release" payment mechanism that
ensures funds are only transferred to sellers or providers upon successful
fulfillment.
• Scheduling & Tracking: A calendar system to prevent double-booking and
real-time status tracking for both orders and service inquiries.
1.6 Project Aim and Objectives
1.6.1 Aim
The primary aim of this project is to develop a comprehensive, user-friendly web-based
platform, Nestora, which bridges the gap between customers, service providers, and
product sellers by facilitating integrated home service management and secure e-
commerce transactions.

1.6.2 Objectives
1. To implement a secure role-based access control system with administrator
verification for service providers and product sellers before their listings become
visible.
2. To enable customers to browse products and services, place orders, submit
inquiries, manage service offers, and track orders with real-time status updates.
3. To allow service providers and product sellers to manage listings, bookings,

4. To allow service providers and product sellers to manage listings, bookings,

1.7 Significance of the Study
The significance of Nestora lies in its ability to bring together service providers, product
sellers, customers, and administrators within a single digital platform that simplifies
communication, improves coordination, and increases transparency. In many existing
workflows, service inquiries, product orders, scheduling, and user verification are
handled through separate tools or manual processes, which often cause delays,
miscommunication, and difficulty in tracking progress. Nestora addresses these issues
by providing a centralized environment where all major interactions can be managed
more efficiently.
For customers, the system offers a convenient way to explore services and products,
submit inquiries or orders, and follow up on ongoing transactions without relying on
fragmented communication channels. For service providers, it provides a structured
method to manage service listings, respond to inquiries, organize schedules, and
maintain project visibility. For product sellers, Nestora supports inventory control,
order handling, and stock monitoring, which helps reduce errors and improve
operational efficiency. Administrators benefit from a clear oversight mechanism for
user verification, platform monitoring, and overall system control.
The study is also significant because it combines multiple business functions into one
integrated solution rather than treating services and products as separate systems. This
improves scalability, usability, and long-term maintainability. By using modern web
technologies and secure authentication methods, Nestora supports a more reliable and
user-friendly digital ecosystem that can be adapted for future enhancement and wider
practical use.

2. Methodology
2.1 Introduction
The Waterfall model is employed for the development of Nestora because the system
follows a structured, sequential process. Given that the project requirements including
the verification system, inventory management, and service inquiry workflows are
clearly defined in the system functions, this model ensures that each phase is completed
before the next begins. This approach allows for effective time management and clear
division of labor among team members during the development of the integrated home
service platform.
2.1.1 Application of the Waterfall Model in Nestora
Phase 1: Requirement Definition
In this initial phase, user needs were identified through analysis of the home service
and e-commerce landscape to understand the gap between service providers and
product sellers. The challenges related to provider verification, real-time inventory
tracking, and secure payment handling were focused on and understood. Additionally,
existing digital tools were analyzed to identify functional gaps, particularly regarding
integrated scheduling and product expiry management. The requirements were then
documented and categorized according to user roles: Customer, Service Provider,
Seller, and Admin. Requirement engineering is considered a fundamental activity in
any plan-driven process such as the Waterfall model.
• Conducted analysis of user challenges related to:
Provider verification and approval
Real-time stock and inventory management
Service scheduling and double-booking prevention
Secure payment processing and fulfillment
• Documented requirements clearly.
• Categorized requirements by user roles:
Customer
Service Provider
Product Seller
Admin (Operations and Financial)
• Emphasized importance of requirement engineering in plan-driven (Waterfall)
development process.
4

--- PAGE 12 ---
Phase 2: System Design
Based on the gathered requirements, the system architecture was designed. This
included the creation of:
• Wireframes for user dashboards and system workflows for each role, including
the inquiry-to-offer status transition.
• UML diagrams such as Use Case, Class, and Sequence Diagrams to visually
map the logical flow of the notification and order systems.
In the Waterfall model, system design is carried out with a clear separation of concerns,
and the system structure is defined before any implementation is begun.
Phase 3: Implementation
The development process was carried out in modular phases.
• The frontend was built using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React.js to deliver
dynamic, responsive user interfaces for customers, product sellers, and service
providers.
• The backend was developed using pure PHP 8.4 to handle business logic, such
as automated expiry alerts, user verification workflows, and payment holds.
• All data was managed in MySQL, and the system was run locally using
XAMPP during development.
In accordance with the Waterfall model, each specification was translated directly into
working code, reflecting the structured nature of this phase.
Phase 4: Testing
After development, comprehensive testing procedures were carried out to ensure
reliability and correctness.
• Unit Testing: Verified each module (e.g., expiry logic, stock auto-deduction)
individually.
• Integration Testing: Ensured that modules such as verification approval, service
inquiries, and payment processing worked together seamlessly.
• System Testing: Evaluated the entire system for compliance with the initial
requirements.
According to Sommerville, testing in the Waterfall model is conducted to ensure that
each component functions as designed before deployment is initiated.

Phase 5:Maintenance
The system was actively monitored for bugs, performance issues, and user feedback.
• Updates were implemented, security vulnerabilities were patched, and features
were enhanced based on real usage data and logs collected from the admin
audit logs.
• Regular maintenance was carried out to ensure continued usability and data

In the Waterfall model, post-deployment maintenance is treated as a separate, well-

2.2 Requirements Identification
2.2.1 Functional and Non-Functional Requirements
Functional Requirements
1. User Registration and Login
• The platform provides a function for users to register with a valid
email and password.
• Users can choose their specific account type: Customer, Service
Provider, or Product Seller.
• The system redirects users to their respective control panels upon
successful authentication.
2. Verification & Approval
• Administrators must review and approve registrations before listings can
go live.
• The system restricts unverified accounts from being visible or active on
the platform.
• Applicants are notified automatically regarding their verification status.
3. Inventory Management
• The system auto-deducts stock quantity immediately after each order is
dispatched.
• It triggers low stock alerts when quantities fall below a seller-defined
threshold.
• The platform tracks product expiry dates and flags or removes expired
listings automatically.
4. Inquiry & Offer Workflow
• Customer inquiries must be routed accurately to the correct service
provider.
6

--- PAGE 14 ---
• The system tracks inquiry status through stages: Sent, Reviewed, and
Accepted or Rejected.
• Accepted offers are linked automatically to the scheduling and fulfillment
workflow.
5. Payment and Order Management
• Payments are processed securely at checkout and held until fulfillment is
confirmed.
• The system generates unique order IDs and updates status from Placed to
Delivered.
• The platform prevents the purchase of out-of-stock products to maintain
data integrity.
6. Scheduling & Tracking
• The system manages provider availability calendars to prevent double-
booking of time slots.
• It tracks and displays real-time order and service status to the customer.
• Scheduled appointments are confirmed to both the provider and the
customer upon booking.
Non-functional Requirements
1. Usability
• The system should feature a simple, professional, and user-friendly interface.
• Pages must load quickly with minimal response times for a smooth user
experience.
2. Scalability
• The architecture should support future expansion in both feature sets and user
volume.
3. Maintainability
• The backend code must be well-organized and modular to allow for easy
updates.
4. Data Privacy & Security
• All user data must be encrypted and accessible only to authorized personnel.
Reliability
• The system must work consistently and perform regular data backups to
prevent loss.
5. Reusability
• The system components and code modules should be designed in a reusable
manner to support future feature development and reduce development time.
6. Testability
• The system should be designed in a modular and structured manner to allow
efficient testing, debugging, and validation of individual components and
system functions.

2.2.2 User Roles
System Admin
• Manage all user accounts including adding, updating, or deleting profiles.
• Review and approve Service Provider and Product Seller registrations before
they are active.
• Monitor system-wide operations, inquiries, and platform interactions through
audit logs.
• Enforce role-based access control and restrict unverified accounts.
• Manage secure online payments and transaction records.
• Oversee the hold-and-release payment mechanism to ensure fulfillment.
• Generate financial reports, product sales history, and transaction analytics.
• Track marketplace operations, payment statuses, and sales income.
Service Provider
• Create a professional profile and undergo an admin verification process.
• Manage a dedicated scheduling calendar to set availability and prevent double-
booking.
• Receive real-time notifications for customer inquiries and send formal service
offers.
• View profile analytics and review request data to improve service visibility.
Product Seller
• List home products and manage an automated inventory system.
• Monitor real-time stock levels and receive alerts for low-stock items.
• Track product expiry dates and receive automated notifications for near-expiry
stock.
• View detailed sales history reports and manage product-specific analytics.
Customer
• Register and log in to browse both bulk products and professional services.
• Place service inquiries and receive real-time status updates from providers.
• Complete secure payments at checkout and track delivery status in real time.
• Rate products and services after a confirmed delivery or completed service.

3.Implementation
3.1 Project Planning and Initial Design
To ensure a smooth development process, the Nestora system was first planned by
preparing essential design diagrams such as the Use Case Diagram, Class Diagram, and
ER Diagram. These diagrams helped define the interactions between users and the
system, the relationships among entities, and the overall database structure. They
provided a clear development roadmap and ensured consistency between the
requirements, system design, and implementation. By using these diagrams at the early
stage, the project team was able to organize the core functions of the platform in a
structured way before beginning the actual development.
3.2 User Interface Development
The frontend of Nestora was developed using React.js, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn user
interface components to create a responsive and modern user experience. Each page
was built as a reusable component to improve scalability, maintainability, and
consistency across the platform. The user interface was designed to support different
user roles, including customers, service providers, product sellers, and administrators.
The main interface sections include:
• Home Page: Provides an overview of the platform, featured services and
products, and quick access to the main features.
• Authentication Pages: Allows users to register, log in, and verify their
accounts using OTP-based verification.
• Customer Dashboard: Enables customers to browse services and products,
place inquiries or orders, and track their activities.
• Service Provider Dashboard: Allows service providers to manage service
listings, respond to inquiries, and organize schedules.
• Product Seller Dashboard: Enables sellers to manage inventory, monitor stock
availability, and handle product orders.
• Admin Dashboard: Supports user verification, platform monitoring, and
management of system activities.
• Profile and Management Pages: Allow users to update personal information
and manage account-related settings.
The interface was designed with usability and accessibility in mind, so users can
interact with the system easily from both desktop and mobile devices.
3.3 Backend Development
The backend of Nestora was implemented using PHP as the server-side language and
MySQL as the database management system. The backend handles user authentication,
role-based access, data processing, and communication between the frontend and the
database. It was developed in a way that supports security, reliability, and smooth
system performance.
• Authentication Module: Supports secure registration, login, and OTP-based
email verification for new users.
14

--- PAGE 22 ---
• Role Management Module: Assigns users to their respective roles, such as
customer, provider, seller, or administrator.
• User Verification Module: Allows administrators to verify and manage
platform users before full access is granted.
• Inquiry and Scheduling Module: Handles customer inquiries, service provider
responses, and appointment coordination.
• Inventory Management Module: Stores and updates product stock details,
item availability, and related data for sellers.
• Order and Payment Module: Manages order processing and integrates
payment gateway support for secure transactions.
• Notification and Record Management: Stores system activities, user actions,
and transaction details for future reference.
The backend was structured to support secure data handling and efficient
communication between all system components. This ensured that Nestora could
perform its functions in a reliable and organized manner.
3.4 System Integration and Workflow
After the frontend and backend modules were developed, they were integrated to create
a complete working system. The workflow of Nestora begins when a user registers an
account and verifies it through OTP. After authentication, the user is redirected to the
appropriate dashboard based on their role. Customers can browse and interact with
services or products, service providers can manage inquiries and schedules, product
sellers can control inventory and handle orders, and administrators can supervise the
platform. This integration allows all major functions to operate within one centralized
system.

4. Testing and Evaluation
4.1 Testing Approach
Several testing approaches were applied:
• Unit Testing: Tested individual functions and components, such as email OTP
verification code generation/expiry, password hashing, form inputs, and
notification triggers.
• Integration Testing: Verified interactions between modules (e.g., customer
submitting a pro application ↔ admin panel displaying the request ↔ approval
generating a Stripe checkout link).
• System Testing: Ensured the entire platform worked as a whole, covering end-
to-end user journeys from registration, dashboard navigation, to payment
processing.
• User Acceptance Testing (UAT): Simulated real-world usage by customers,
service providers, product sellers, and platform administrators to validate
usability, layout responsiveness, and role access constraints.

4.3 Evaluation
• Performance: The system responded smoothly under concurrent user testing,
with fast page load times using Vite on the frontend and lightweight PHP/PDO
queries on the backend.
• Security: OTP email verification, secure session-based authentication,
password hashing, and role-based access control (RBAC) ensured that user data
and access are safe.
• Usability: The clean, responsive interface powered by React and Tailwind CSS
worked seamlessly across desktop and mobile devices. Multi-step pro
application and payment flows were intuitive and easy to navigate.
• Reliability: Precise database transactions, automated stock level updates, and
robust webhook integration with Stripe ensured a highly reliable booking,
checkout, and payment cycle with minimal errors.
Overall, testing confirmed that Nestora met its functional and non-functional
requirements, providing a secure, efficient, and user-friendly platform.

5. Conclusion
5.1 Conclusion
Nestora was developed as a centralized web-based platform to improve the way
customers, service providers, product sellers, and administrators manage service-
related and product-related activities. The project addressed the limitations of
traditional manual and fragmented systems by providing secure registration, user
verification, role-based access, service and product management, inquiry handling,
scheduling, inventory tracking, and payment processing within a single integrated
environment.
Throughout the development process, the system was designed to improve efficiency,
transparency, and convenience for all user categories. Customers can easily access
services and products, while service providers and product sellers can manage their
operations more effectively through dedicated dashboards. Administrators are also
given the necessary control to monitor the platform and verify users to maintain
security and reliability. By using modern technologies such as React.js, Tailwind CSS,
shadcn, PHP, and MySQL, Nestora offers a responsive, scalable, and secure solution.
In conclusion, Nestora successfully fulfills its main objectives by simplifying
communication, improving coordination, and reducing the complexity of managing
services and products in one place. The system provides a practical and user-friendly
solution that can be further expanded with future enhancements to meet growing user
needs and improve overall performance.
